Crystal structure

CaCO₃, orthorhombic.

Aragonite is one of the natural polymorphs of calcium carbonate. Its orthorhombic lattice differs from calcite and can influence density, solubility and process behavior.

Lifecycle strategy

Building an evidence-based lifecycle strategy.

Aragonite participates in the natural marine carbonate cycle. Any source-renewal, emissions or carbon-benefit claim for a delivered product still requires defined boundaries and independent lifecycle evidence.

Marine formation

The material originates in naturally formed Bahamian oolitic deposits.

Responsible sourcing

Source, disturbance and chain-of-custody questions should be documented at the operating level.

Lifecycle evidence

A third-party LCA is needed before making a verified carbon-footprint or carbon-negative claim.

Research pathways

Potential climate applications remain research topics and require project-specific validation.

AragoCor does not present the delivered mineral as verified carbon-negative. Lifecycle performance must be established with transparent, third-party-reviewed evidence.
