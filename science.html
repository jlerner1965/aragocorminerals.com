What it is

Biogenic oolitic aragonite, in one view.

Oolitic aragonite is a naturally precipitated, ocean-derived form of calcium carbonate from the Great Bahama Bank. Its spherical ooids are built from interlocking acicular crystals, giving high surface area and microporosity — the orthorhombic aragonite polymorph, distinct from the calcite that makes up quarried limestone.

Aragonite vs calcite / GCC

Where it wins — and where it doesn't.

Higher reactivity is the through-line: an advantage in water treatment (faster dissolution), differentiated in cement, and a disadvantage in polymers. It describes how the mineral behaves — not a claim that aragonite is universally better than calcite.
